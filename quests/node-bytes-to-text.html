00 Explorable explanation · with Node internals

What a Buffer really is —
and why a stream splits the € in two.

A Buffer is a view over raw memory. A stream moves it in byte-sized chunks. Put those two facts together and a single three-byte character can be sliced across a chunk boundary — producing mojibake the instant you decode each chunk on its own. Press play to trace the string "café €99" through the seam, click any node, and open “under the hood” for the Node internals and the Unicode rules they implement.
